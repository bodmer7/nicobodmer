Geo quiz

Guess places on the map.

Situation
You know your own region from the bike and the train window. On a blank map you still cannot find it.
Approach
A quiz app where you tap places on an unlabelled aerial map. Scoring is based on the distance to the correct point.
Result
Live web app with eight questions per round, aerial imagery from swisstopo and its own logo.

Technology: Vite, TypeScript, Leaflet, OpenStreetMap, swisstopo, GitHub Actions and Pages

Health dashboard

Sports watch data at a glance.

Situation
The sports watch collects health data every day, and it stays locked inside the manufacturer's app.
Approach
A local MCP server makes the data available read-only. Every morning it is turned into a dashboard automatically.
Result
20 read-only queries and a dashboard covering readiness, sleep, achievements and weight goal. The screenshot shows sample data.

Technology: MCP, Python, SQLite, Jinja2, Playwright

In progress: a forecasting app.
